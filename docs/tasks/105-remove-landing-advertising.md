# Task 105 — Remove advertising from the landing promise

## Problem

The landing page still presents advertising intelligence as the third product
pillar even though advertising is not part of the current customer-facing
report navigation. This overpromises a capability and conflicts with the
Competitors, Products, and Benchmark experience.

## Decision

- Replace the Ads pillar with Benchmark in English and Arabic.
- Describe the benchmark through the useful dimensions already shown in saved
  reports: product access, information quality, trust, and purchase experience.
- Remove advertising references from the hero and collection-method copy.
- Keep the existing three-column visual composition and responsive behavior.

## Acceptance

- The landing page contains no visible Ads or advertising-signal promise.
- The three pillars are Competitors, Products, and Benchmark.
- English and Arabic copy communicate the same product scope.
- Existing report creation and navigation behavior is unchanged.
- Build, lint, focused tests, strict review, deployment, and live verification pass.
